    What I want to work on in graduate school

    I want to join a funded graduate program (MS or PhD) that combines wet-lab microbiology with computational methods, and I am open to groups working on infectious disease, oncology, immunology or cardiovascular questions.

    Antiviral and anticancer drug discovery

    Pairing in silico screening (docking, drug-design tools and dynamics) with biochemical approaches. This builds on my published antiviral and phytocompound work and my Zika NS2B-NS3 peptide project.

    HPV-related and gynecologic oncology

    Building on my publications in Frontiers in Oncology and my review work on HPV and the vaginal microbiome.

    Infectious disease and molecular diagnostics

    Using the PCR, biochemical testing and pathogen identification skills from my clinical microbiology internship and research assistant role.

    Toxicology and nanobiotechnology

    Following on from my clinical toxicology case work and my presentation on microbially synthesized biopolymer nanocomposites.
